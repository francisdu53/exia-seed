| 0.10.0 | 23/11/2026 | repository | **Two memories, one web** (`deux_memoires.py`, `deux_memoires_poids.json`): the automatic recall reads the consolidated memories AND the raw conversation, with no model at recall time. Setting `rappel_moteur: deux_memoires`; default `ancien` — installing 0.10.0 changes nothing until it is switched, and setting it back is the rollback. Memories web: each lit fact gets the sum of its cues in log-odds (BM25 rarity, coverage of the message, named page, concept rarity, rule, time window), weights fitted on benchmark TUNING sets only; pseudo-relevance feedback on memories. Raw web: turns of two replies (one if they exceed 600 characters), never a retracted line, never the last 3 hours; neighbouring turns follow. Both lists merged by log-odds; kept if the chance of helping is ≥ `deux_memoires_seuil` (0.05), at most `deux_memoires_max` (10), 1,200-token guard, nothing repeated within 3 hours. **Abstention rule** at the top of the block (`deux_memoires_regle`); the agent's own lines in raw passages are marked « (you) »; **corrector drawn from the memory** (`deux_memoires_correcteur`: a typo is mapped to the closest word the memory knows; a known word is never touched); accents folded on both sides; ties broken by a fixed order (same message, same memory → same memories across restarts). Evaluated on LoCoMo and LongMemEval with sealed sets and a free evidence sensor (`docs/22`): on the fused verdict sets, budget 450 tokens, reader and judge GPT-6.1 Terra (not comparable to public leaderboards) — LoCoMo 63.1 % vs hybrid RAG 58.2 % (p = 0.001) and the watch 43.0 %; LongMemEval 58.9 % vs embeddings on memories 54.9 % (not significant) and the watch 48.2 %; with the abstention rule, traps answered « not mentioned » 12 → 140 of 164, at a cost of about 6 points on ordinary LoCoMo questions. Recall 5–30 ms and ≈ 350 MB, against ≈ 300 ms and 2.4–2.9 GB for bge-m3 retrieval on CPU. Damaged questions: 50 % typos on LoCoMo 63.3 → 77.7 % with the corrector. Not solved: interference when one person's memory grows (evidence in the block 93.6 % → 69.1 % from 1 to 500 conversations; two candidate fixes rejected on the test set). Not yet switched on for the agent: real-life validation pending. Also: `docs/21` §3 corrected (public-benchmark figures re-measured with the released 0.9.16); bench scripts in `banc/eval/`. `tests/test_deux_memoires.py` (20). | `docs/22-two-memories-one-web.md` |
